✏️ CSS Essay Writing Tips

📏 Length

Aim for 1,000–1,500 words. Too short shows insufficient depth; too long wastes exam time.

🏗️ Structure

Introduction → Thesis → 4–6 body paragraphs → Conclusion. Each paragraph = one argument.

🎯 Thesis Statement

End your introduction with a clear, specific thesis that previews your main argument.

🔗 Transitions

Use transitional phrases: "Furthermore," "However," "In contrast," "Consequently."

📊 Evidence

Support arguments with statistics, examples, case studies, and scholarly references where possible.

⚖️ Balance

Present multiple perspectives. Show awareness of counter-arguments before refuting them.
