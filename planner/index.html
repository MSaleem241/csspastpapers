📌 Planning Tips

Daily newspaper — Read Dawn every morning without exception. This alone builds 40% of your current affairs knowledge.
Write daily — Even 30 minutes of essay or précis writing every day compounds dramatically over months.
Spaced revision — Revisit each subject every 10–14 days. Forgetting is the enemy; revision is the solution.
One rest day — A proper weekly rest day improves retention and prevents burnout.
Track completion — Use the Roadmap to mark topics done. Visible progress is motivating.
