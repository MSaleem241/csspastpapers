📋 Rules of Précis Writing

✂️

One-Third Length

A précis should be approximately one-third the word count of the original passage.

🖊️

Your Own Words

Do not copy sentences from the original. Rephrase all ideas in your own language.

🎯

Main Ideas Only

Retain all essential points. Remove illustrations, examples, and all repetitions.

📐

Continuous Prose

Write in continuous paragraphs, not bullet points. Usually one or two cohesive paragraphs.

👤

Third Person

Always write in the third person, even if the original passage uses first person.

⏰

Past Tense

Use simple past tense throughout your précis, regardless of the original's tense.

📝

Give a Title

In CSS, you must provide a suitable title for your précis. Make it concise and accurate.

🚫

No Personal Opinion

A précis is a factual summary. Do not add your own opinions, comments, or interpretations.
