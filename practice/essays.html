✏️ CSS Essay Writing Tips

📏 Target Length

Aim for 1,000–1,400 words. Too short shows insufficient depth; too long risks losing quality under time pressure.

🏗️ Structure is Everything

Introduction (thesis) → 4–6 body paragraphs (one argument each) → Counter-argument → Conclusion.

🎯 Thesis First

End your introduction with a specific, arguable thesis. "This essay will discuss X" is not a thesis. Take a position.

🔗 Transitions

Use: "Furthermore," "However," "In contrast," "Consequently," "This suggests that" to link paragraphs coherently.

📊 Evidence

Support every argument with a statistic, example, or case study. The examiner rewards informed, evidence-based arguments.

⚖️ Show Balance

Include at least one counter-argument paragraph. Acknowledging the other side strengthens, not weakens, your essay.

⏱️ Time Management

Allocate: 10 min planning → 100 min writing → 20 min review and editing. Never start without a plan.

🖊️ Formal Register

Avoid contractions, slang, and passive voice overuse. Write clearly and precisely — ornate complexity hides weak thinking.
