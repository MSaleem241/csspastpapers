📋 Rules of Précis Writing

✂️
One-Third Length

A précis should be approximately one-third the length of the original passage.

🖊️
Own Words

Do not copy sentences from the original. Rephrase all ideas in your own language.

🎯
Main Ideas Only

Retain all essential points. Remove illustrations, examples, and repetitions.

📐
Coherent Prose

Write in continuous prose, not bullet points. Usually one or two paragraphs.

🔤
Third Person

Always write in the third person, even if the original uses first person.

⏰
Past Tense

Use simple past tense throughout your précis regardless of the original's tense.
