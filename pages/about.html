About csspastpapers.com

Pakistan's most complete free CSS preparation platform

Our Mission

csspastpapers.com exists to give every CSS aspirant in Pakistan — regardless of location, resources, or access to coaching — the preparation tools they need to compete on merit and clear the examination.

The CSS examination is one of the most intellectually demanding tests in Pakistan. It rewards sustained, structured preparation. But quality preparation resources have historically been expensive, scattered, and unequal in access. We are changing that.

What We Offer

  • Past Papers 2010–2024 — all compulsory subjects in one place
  • 200+ Essay Topics with CSS-specific hints and category filters
  • 150+ Précis Passages at three difficulty levels with instant feedback
  • 1000+ MCQs across all compulsory subjects with scoring and session tracking
  • 100 Expert Articles covering strategy, subject preparation, and skills
  • Study Roadmap — mark topics complete across the entire CSS syllabus
  • Study Planner — personalized weekly schedules based on your timeline
  • Analytics — topic frequency and trend analysis from 15 years of papers
  • Essay Analyzer — instant structural feedback on your writing
  • Question Analyzer — detect keywords and get answer frameworks

Everything is free. Everything is accessible without registration.

Our Principles

  • No registration required — your progress is saved locally in your browser
  • No ads — zero advertising content on any page
  • No paywalls — every feature is free, forever
  • Quality over quantity — every article, question, and passage is written for CSS, not repurposed from generic content
