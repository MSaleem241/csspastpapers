✍️ CSS Answer Writing Tips

⏱️ Time Allocation

Optional paper: ~30 mins per answer. Spend 5 mins planning before writing.

📏 Ideal Length

CSS subjective answers: 500–700 words for 20-mark questions.

🎯 First Paragraph

Open with a definition, scope statement, and brief preview of your argument.

📊 Evidence

Every argument needs a fact, example, or data point. Avoid unsupported assertions.

⚖️ Balance

Even for "Discuss" questions — present both sides before your conclusion.

🔚 Conclusion

Synthesize your argument. Don't just summarize — offer a forward-looking insight.
